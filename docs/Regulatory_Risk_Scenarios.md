# Regulatory Risk Scenarios and Mitigation

## Take Rate Compression (Highest-impact risk)

**Current assumption:** 1.2% constant through M36 (CBUAE regulatory ceiling)

**Risk trigger:** Aani instant payment migration and CBUAE corridor relaxation

**Scenario modeling:**
- Base: 1.2% flat
- Regulatory pressure: 1.2% to 1.0% at Month 18 (20 basis points compression)
- Severe: 1.2% to 0.80% by Month 24 (40 basis points compression)

**Impact:** A 20 basis points compression reduces Year 3 revenue by $5.4M. See the 21_Scenarios sheet in the model for live sensitivity.

**Mitigation:**
1. Diversify revenue toward subscription, raising attach from 18% to 25% or more
2. Bring the lending product online earlier to capture higher margin, roughly 14% versus 8% interchange
3. Monitor Aani adoption metrics quarterly and keep contingency pricing in the model

## Lending Yield Regulatory Convergence

**Current assumption:** 1.1% per month (14.1% annualized) constant through M36

**Risk trigger:** CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation rate caps

**Scenario modeling:**
- Base: 1.1% per month (14.1% p.a.)
- Regulatory convergence: 1.1% to 0.917% per month (11% p.a.) at Month 18
- Full rate cap: 1.1% to 0.75% per month (9% p.a.) at Month 24

**Impact:** A 100 basis points yield compression reduces Year 3 revenue by about $3 to $4M and moves EBITDA margin to about -20% to -22%.

**Mitigation:**
1. Model origination fees as a separate revenue stream, since one-time fees may not be rate capped
2. Prioritize loan volume growth over margin in the near term
3. Engage CBUAE early on rate methodology and seek clarity on effective rate limits
